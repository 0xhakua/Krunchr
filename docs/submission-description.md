# Krunchr — Submission Description

Every year, millions of self-employed Filipino freelancers face the same dread: not one tax return, but up to eight, filed in a strict legal sequence, each with its own deadline and a web of interacting rules where a single wrong move triggers surcharges, daily interest, and penalties. Most can't afford a quarterly accountant, so they file by hand and hope — and even when they get it right, all they have to show for it is a scanned PDF that anyone could have altered. Krunchr turns that anxiety into something that simply feels handled: a freelancer uploads their income certificates, and the compliance engine computes their entire tax year, walks them through every required return in the correct order, and generates ready-to-file documents. Then comes the part only Stellar makes possible — each filed return is cryptographically anchored on the Stellar network, producing a tamper-proof, publicly verifiable receipt that a bank, an embassy, or an auditor can confirm in seconds without trusting anyone's servers. This is a genuine "real-world access" use case: not speculation, but everyday compliance made trustworthy. It showcases Stellar's low-cost anchoring to solve a real problem in a market where Stellar already has payment-rail traction, and — as far as we could find — it's the first tax-compliance product built on Stellar, opening a path toward deeper integration through anchors, KYC standards, and portable, privacy-preserving compliance credentials.

**For reviewers:** Krunchr is built for the Local Finance & Real World Access track. The demo moment to watch for is end-to-end and works today: onboard, upload income certificates, watch all returns compute in sequence, file one, and verify its Stellar-anchored receipt via a scannable QR — roughly thirty seconds from filing to third-party verification. As you review, we'd encourage you to weigh three things: (1) problem-solution fit — this is a painful, high-stakes, recurring problem for a large underserved population, not a manufactured use case; (2) the Stellar impact — the anchored receipt is the trust unlock that turns a private chore into something institutions can rely on, and our roadmap deepens this from simple hash-anchoring into on-chain attestation registries and verifiable compliance credentials; and (3) honesty of scope — the flat-rate filing path is fully working, and we're candid about what's next (broader tax scenarios, mainnet anchoring, and payment-rail integrations). We've also published a fuller pitch, an ecosystem-integration roadmap, and a go-to-market plan spanning the Philippines, APAC, and global markets, which we're happy to walk you through. Thank you for taking the time — we think this is a small, concrete example of Stellar quietly making a real person's life easier.

# Krunchr Pitch Deck — Full-Slide Image Prompts

These correspond to the `[FULL-SLIDE IMAGE — see image prompt #N]` placeholders in
`pitch-deck-draft-Krunchr.pptx`. Paste each prompt into your image model. Target a
16:9 aspect ratio and leave the center/lower area relatively clean so the overlaid
title and text stay legible.

## Image Prompt #1 — Title / Hook slide

A calm, confident hero image for a fintech product called "Krunchr." A young
Filipino freelancer sits relaxed at a small home desk with a laptop and a phone,
a warm morning light coming through a window, expression of relief and quiet
control — the feeling of a hard chore now "handled." Subtle abstract motif of
neat, orderly document cards and a small glowing verification checkmark floating
near the laptop, hinting at automated tax filing. Brand palette of deep teal and
fresh mint accents against soft neutral tones. Modern, clean editorial-tech
illustration style, shallow depth of field, uncluttered composition with generous
negative space in the center and lower third for overlaid title text. 16:9,
high resolution, no visible text or logos.

## Image Prompt #2 — Problem slide

A conceptual editorial image evoking the anxiety and complexity of Philippine tax
filing for a self-employed freelancer. A single overwhelmed person at a desk half-
buried under a cascading, tangled stack of official-looking tax forms, calendars
with red deadline circles, and stamped papers — a sense of a maze of paperwork and
looming penalties. Muted, slightly tense color grade with deep teal shadows and
warning-amber highlights on the deadline marks. Cinematic, moody, dramatic side
lighting; the paperwork chaos concentrated toward the top and left, leaving clean
darker negative space on the right and lower area for overlaid bullet text. 16:9,
high resolution, no readable text on the documents, no logos.
